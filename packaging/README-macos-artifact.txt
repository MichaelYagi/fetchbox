Open the .dmg and drag Fetchbox to Applications, then open it from there. It runs in
the menu bar (the blue box icon at the top right, no Dock icon) and opens Fetchbox in
your browser. Click the icon to open Fetchbox again, pause downloads, stop or restart
it, open its data or logs folder, start it at login, or quit.

The app isn't signed, so the first time macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "can't be
checked for malicious software". Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click
"Open Anyway" next to Fetchbox, or right-click the app > Open > Open. Your data is kept
in ~/Library/Application Support/Fetchbox. To uninstall, quit Fetchbox and move it to
the Bin; delete that folder too if you want your settings gone.
