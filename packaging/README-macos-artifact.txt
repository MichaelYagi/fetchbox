Move Fetchbox.app to Applications and open it. It runs in the menu bar (the blue box
icon at the top right, no Dock icon) and opens Fetchbox in your browser. Click the icon
to open Fetchbox again, stop or restart it, open its data or logs folder, or quit.

The app isn't signed, so the first time macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "can't be
checked for malicious software". Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click
"Open Anyway" next to Fetchbox, or right-click the app > Open > Open. Your data is kept
in ~/Library/Application Support/Fetchbox.
